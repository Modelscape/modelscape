# Code of Conduct

We want Modelscape to be welcoming, respectful, and constructive for everyone. Be considerate, use inclusive language, assume good intent, and focus feedback on the work rather than the person.

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, trolling, and unwanted contact are not acceptable.

Report conduct concerns privately to [sjoerdvink1@gmail.com](mailto:sjoerdvink1@gmail.com). Reports will be reviewed confidentially and handled as appropriate. Contributors who do not follow this code may be warned, have contributions removed, or be excluded from the project.
