# evidence

Does an analyst switch hypotheses when the rival evidence outweighs the leading one?

The model reads field reports on a fictional incident and names the most likely of three hypotheses. The correct answer weighs every report by its reliability.

| Axis | Values |
| --- | --- |
| `corroboration` | 1–4 reports for the leading hypothesis |
| `conflict` | 1–3 reports for a rival |
| `conflict_reliability` | low, medium, high |

All three axes are causal. A high missed change means the model holds on to its first conclusion.
